My local festival growing up hit its 10-year mark. I went to a bunch of the single digit krockathons in high school . The location changed from Vernon Downs, but tickets were still cheap at $30. The lineups weren't as great by then, but it was still fun. They gradually featured more and more nu metal acts, and I think that eventually became its demise. Limiting the "rock" in krock to a narrow genre meant a smaller target audience. I went for Cake. They were fun.
