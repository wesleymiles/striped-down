What a bill. Two of my faves.


## Midlake

Midlake were touring *Antiphon* (2013), their fourth record and first without frontman Tim Smith. I came in open-minded — *Antiphon* and the album before it (*The Courage of Others*, 2010) were both okay but neither lived up to my first love: *The Trials of Van Occupanther*.

## Band of Horses

Band of Horses leaned heavily on the classics — *Everything All the Time* and *Cease to Begin* era material. Not much from the 2012 *Mirage Rock* record. No complaints.
