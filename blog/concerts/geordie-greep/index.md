I was excited for this one. [The new sound](https://geordiegreep.bandcamp.com/album/the-new-sound) was a top album of the year for me. He didn't disappoint. Much of the show was improvisational. He extended songs and created new ones, embodying Frank Zappa orchestrating his whims. His fast riffs and grooves were even faster live. It was an experience.
