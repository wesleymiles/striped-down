Made it to the Newport Folk once while living in the boston area. I didn't really have a pulse for the Rhode Island scene. Went for Ryan Adams, Jenny Lewis, and Band of Horses. Those three lept past expectations. Crowd stunners. 

I posted up to watch the Sun Kil Moon set. I was only vaguely aware of the band at the time, and that the front man Mark Kozelek was in Almost Famous. I left early due to him being a vibe killer. He awkwardly and belligerently yelled at some listeners. The music is slow and calm, and he was confrontational and couldn't hide his jerkness behind it.
