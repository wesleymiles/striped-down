## Rogue Wave

It was sort of an odd matchup. Rogue Wave represented the 2010's era of dance indie, with the hit [Good Morning-The Future](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WhZEoNWZ5fU), which was an honest banger. Their older catalog was more of a fit; more along the lines of their SubPop peers The Shins.
