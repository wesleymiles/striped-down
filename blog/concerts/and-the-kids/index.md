RIP [Radiobean](https://www.radiobean.com/). They closed their doors 9/8/2026 due to slimy landlords and [insurance companies](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/music/musicnews/burlingtons-radio-bean-to-close-next-week/). The tiny music club was a gem and was connected to an eclectic eatery. You could feel the community when you walked in. These photos are terrible but at least capture the DIY-nature of the club. The three piece rocked.
