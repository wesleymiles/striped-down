## The Sadies

Toronto country-rock openers. In 2022 the four-piece became a three-piece as the engine of the band Dallas Good died at 48. The band still rocks on. Upright bass, Gretsch guitar showing off fast licks.

## Kurt Vile and The Violators

Listening to Ram Dass on the way to the KV show felt right. Kurt’s energy is so low-ego, he radiates letting-go to the crowd through his slacker unconcern. My mind was primed with thoughts of dropping the ego-world and entering the soul-world as the show started. Kurt's entire vibe maintained those thoughts.
